\slidetitle{08 / straight talk}{What we are not claiming --- yet}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\textwidth}\vspace{0pt}
{\fontsize{9.6}{13}\selectfont\color{Ink}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Scope of the splits.} Solubility and $T_g$ use group-safe splits (leave-solute-out, scaffold LOSO). Density and refractive index (polymer ML) use random/source-stratified splits and demonstrate \emph{within-chemotype} performance. The $T_g$ lift is single-corpus: cross-corpus transfer was tested, failed, and is queued behind a second rights-clean corpus.
  \item \textbf{Signing tiers.} The solvency engine carries trust-anchored Phase-0 signatures; most other receipts are signed with the audit-only development key --- byte-stable and replayable, but the trust root is the founder-signed manifest.
  \item \textbf{Hansen comparator vintage.} The $-$62\% is against the classical single-$\delta$ treatment; a head-to-head against a modern group-contribution method is queued, not run.
  \item \textbf{Pre-Phase-0.} Promotion requires a human dual-signature ceremony plus external replication; the loop deliberately cannot grant it to itself. Zero modules have been promoted.
