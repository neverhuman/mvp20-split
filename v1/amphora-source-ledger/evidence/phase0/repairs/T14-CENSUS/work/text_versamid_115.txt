                                    VERSAMID® 115

Use                Versamid® 115 is a high viscosity, reactive
                   polyamide resin based on dimerized fatty acid and
                   polyamines. This product is designed for use with
                   solid or liquid epoxy resins to give flexible and
                   resistant thermoset coatings using room temperature
                   cure.

                   Versamid® 115/epoxy coatings are more solvent
                   and chemical resistant than Versamid® 100/epoxy
                   formulations and generally dry faster than
                   Versamid® 125 systems.

                   Versamid® 115/epoxy resin systems are used in
                   maintenance coatings, primers and enamel paint
                   formulations because of their excellent resistance
                   properties and adhesion. This resin is also useful in
                   adhesive     applications.   Versamid®     115     in
                   combination with solid epoxy leads to coatings with
                   superior corrosion resistance.

Miscellaneous            PACKAGING, STORAGE AND HANDLING

                         Versamid® 115 may absorb moisture and carbon
                         dioxide if left in open containers. This may result in
                         an increased viscosity and some foaming when
                         curing epoxy resins. Therefore, it should be kept in
                         tightly closed containers when not in use and stored
                         in a cool, dry place. Properly protected from
                         moisture, the product has a minimum shelf life of
                         one year.

                         Versamid® 115 and epoxy resins should not be
                         mixed until just prior to use because a chemical
                         reaction will take place.

                         Read SAFETY INFORMATION before handling or
                         using product.

                   Safety Information

                   Based on toxicological studies with animals,
                   Versamid® 115 causes eye and skin irritation and
                   may be harmful if swallowed.

                   Do not get product in eyes, on skin or on clothing.
                   Do not inhale product. Use only with adequate
                   ventilation.  Wear     recommended       protective
                   equipment.

                   In case of skin or eye contact, immediately flush with
                   plenty of clean running water for at least 15 minutes
                   while removing contaminated clothing. Call a
                   physician.

                   If swallowed, do not induce vomiting until after
                   consulting a physician. Immediately drink large
                   quantities of milk or water and call a physician.

                   Recommended protective equipment includes
                   chemical goggles, rubber gloves and work clothing
                   plus splash apron and respirator with organic vapor
                   filter unless used with local exhaust. Use an air hood
                   plus other protective equipment if used in a confined
                   area.
