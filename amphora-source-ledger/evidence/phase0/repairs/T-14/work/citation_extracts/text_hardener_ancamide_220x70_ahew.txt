  ANCAMIDE 220X70
  Curing Agent



   DESCRIPTION

   Ancamide 220X70 curing agent is one in a series of standard reactive polyamides intended for use in the
   curing of epoxy resins. Ancamide 220X70 curing agent is cut to 70% solids by weight in xylene.

   Evonik also offers Ancamide 221X70 curing agent which is similar to Ancamide 220X70 in performance.
   Ancamide 221X70 may be a more cost effective alternative for your formulation.
