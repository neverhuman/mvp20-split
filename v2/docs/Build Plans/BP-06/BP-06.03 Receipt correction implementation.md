## Owned boundaries

Graph Runtime contracts already define DomainReceipt and its superseded outcome. Add only shared immutable correction record/reference DTOs there; they provide no shared policy, persistence or permission. Research and Knowledge each implement their own correction policy, private store and authenticated source-selection endpoints. Their source receipt must be obtained from the same owner's already-published output, never trusted as a caller-provided verdict. No service reads another owner's SQL. Runtime behavior/migrations remain unchanged under the owner decision.

A chain is explicitly named within scope/issuer and starts with a published owner receipt. Appending requires the exact expected head and a new source publication with matching issuer, subject and purpose. Same change identity and canonical request replay; changed bytes conflict. Concurrent successors of one head cannot both win. Old chain revisions, source publications and receipts remain immutable. A revision record binds actor, reason, source receipt, prior record reference, new receipt and invalidation notice in one commit.

Chain ordinals are positive safe integers. DomainReceipt revision remains its existing opaque identifier; derive a unique digest from chain/ordinal/source identity, rather than a generic count that could collide with another chain's same-subject revision. Every emitted receipt/notice has a fresh deterministic ID. A correction notice references the prior effective revision and names the replacement revision; it is for future selection and cannot satisfy a wait. The original accepted receipt is not changed. Root records also get an exact chain-bound receipt revision while preserving the original source receipt separately.

Expose explicit current-head selection for campaign authoring and exact-revision reads for replay. Consumers pin the returned effective receipt revision before campaign admission; never repoint a running graph or infer a newer revision during a wait. A later receipt can change supported/contradicted/unknown sufficiency without rewriting an earlier campaign. Category/method/context policy remains the B1 owner assessment policy, not an authority ladder.
