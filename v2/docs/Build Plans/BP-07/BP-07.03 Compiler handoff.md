Bind returned observations to scope, full lock, policy, lifecycle and all structural/scientific/capacity/admission statuses. Candidate observations expire; they are not worker reservations or execution grants. Synthetic-only current Registry policy stays explicit. Runtime repeats its own current admission checks later; Planning cannot transfer an eligibility observation into an execution right.

Before a new dependent handoff, re-read the policy-selected original identities through existing public owner readers. Verify current source processing permission independently of historical byte availability. Compare relevant Research/tool/input identities and selected Runtime locks without treating unrelated inventory or universe changes as blanket staleness. Changed relevant inputs produce a named stale proposal; preserve original bytes and do not repin an active campaign. Missing or contradictory scientific evidence alone remains a visible finding, provided the declared action prerequisites are met.

Revalidate the existing original provider budget and retained reservation/settlement through Planning-owned interfaces. Deriving demand does not reserve again or refund a settled call. Tool execution budgets remain Runtime admission authority. This unit does not invent a new spend policy or automatically call a live provider.

## Transport and durability

Use configured authenticated loopback origins and compiled routes; model text cannot choose hosts, methods, credentials or paths. Bound total time, call count, per-response and aggregate bytes; reject redirects, malformed/duplicate/nonfinite JSON and mismatched receipts. Distinguish absent readback, denial, conflict, explicit rejection and unknown transport outcome. Retain original command bytes for reconciliation; no automatic retry with a new identity after a lost reply.

B2.03 must persist intent and outgoing bytes before any save/validate effect. Peer calls occur outside SQL transactions. A pure request or successful loopback probe is not proof of durable origin. The transport accepts a caller fence guard before sending; the later journal owns that guard and crash recovery. No production coordinator may bypass the journal simply because the client is implemented first.

### Refresh collection scope

The refresh plan reconstructs the combined policy and derives inputs from the validated consumed packet, never from model-provided URLs or hashes. Recheck processing permission for every available Artifact slice consumed by that packet, including roots and source entries; a generated proposal may have used any consumed source. This is a current-use permission check, separate from the narrower semantic read-set. Omitted and originally unavailable Artifact bodies were not consumed and are not fetched. Up to132 distinct identities are divided into at-most128-reference reads sharing one Data budget; no cache or ordinary-content fallback. Owner projections are not expanded into arbitrary transitive sources or represented as new source-rights grants.

Read the original selected Research revisions through the existing bounded public reader. Compare available record content only for policy-referenced identities. New, changed or no-longer-available relevant records require a refreshed proposal; unrelated records do not. If a selected scientific point remains unavailable, preserve both original and fresh gap diagnostics without treating a change of technical gap wording as a new scientific fact or a global veto. This does not establish the latest question revision: latest-selection discovery remains the prospective caller's separate responsibility and is explicitly reported as unverified by these exact reads.

Re-read at most64 selected Registry releases by exact lock, preserving original inventory/exact lane provenance. Use one bounded exact-release window, distinct from the eight-page inventory scan limit. Compare selected release observations and retain explicit failures; do not rescan unrelated inventory. Re-read Runtime's reviewed universe when tools are used and compare selected locks only. A different global universe digest alone is not a stale proposal. Actual schema/type/control-flow compilation remains Runtime's subsequent validation result.

All readers retain their bounded owner budgets and report failures without cross-owner SQL or a transactional snapshot claim. Freshness is an observation at read time, not a distributed lease against later revocation. The final durable coordinator must re-run required fresh preparation before an unissued effect and enforce its own fence; historical reconciliation reads must not silently become new processing use. This unit does not dispatch a command or renew the original campaign's pins.

### Stored origin and combined preparation

Before refreshing or preparing a command, load the original campaign preparation, attempt observation, reservation and settlement through Planning's existing owned journal interfaces. Their actor/scope checks remain authoritative. Compare exact prepared packet/manifest, request/attempt, raw capture and canonical observation to the policy-reviewed proposal. Reconstruct the reservation demand and conservative settled charge; reject missing, unbudgeted, unsettled or mismatched history. Reads do not reserve again, alter a settlement or refund unknown usage. Preserve overage as historical accounting information; this boundary neither grants another model call nor allocates tool-execution funds.

A combined preparer loads owned origin and runs its configured refresh collector, rather than accepting an external ready flag. It revalidates the complete selected eligibility batch and exact outcome coverage and checks positive observations against the clock after refresh. Negative or expired eligibility and relevant input failures/changes yield explicit blocked preparation results; malformed or foreign objects cannot become success. Retain diagnostics and the original proposal for later iteration. Zero-tool drafts require zero Registry observations.

The positive state is ready_to_journal, not saved, compiled or executable. This component sends no save/validate/resolve command and creates no new persistent intent. B2.03 must pin the configuration/check identities and outgoing bytes before effects, obtain/reconcile Registry observations through its guarded workflow and re-run needed current checks before an unissued effect. Its dispatcher cannot treat a retained ready result as a permission lease. Actual owner and SQL proof must distinguish these boundaries from the pure value and scripted-reader tests.
