# BP-07.02 Research proposals and repair admission

- Design status: bounded B2.02 implementation refinement; no implementation credit.
- Last reviewed: `2026-10-04 06:50:51` (America/Chicago).
- Canonical tracker: [BP-07.02](BP-07.02%20Reliable%20text%20decomposition.md).
- Scope: distinct attributed Research proposals and aggregate repair admission. Public Research registration, Data answer publication and accepted Knowledge replay remain B2.03.

## Ordered substeps

B2.02 has three equal substeps within its existing milestone weight: a deterministic question/answer/evidence-need proposal validation; b immutable repair-family membership and atomic aggregate budget/dispatch enforcement; c bounded repair orchestration with exact findings, durable parent lineage and independent process verification. Each starts0%. Substep a has two equal implementation gates: i pure typed proposals and provenance binding, ii bounded public question reads and actual-owner proof. Design text earns no credit. The aggregate-admission design below is a prerequisite to activating a, so later repair behavior is not implicitly authorized by a trace pointer.

## Distinct Research proposals

Use a focused Planning research_proposals module. Questions produce proposed public ResearchQuestion records with stable task/local IDs, exact selected applicability and unique declared criterion IDs. Evidence needs produce proposed public EvidenceNeed records with explicit question identity, nonempty criterion subsets, method and required context. A candidate can refer to a locally proposed question or an exact selected owner question, never both. Existing selected question content must be bound through Research's public read interface before criterion validation; a caller's reference alone proves neither existence nor criteria. Unknown/ambiguous targets remain item findings, and dependent items are withheld without discarding unrelated valid questions.

Research has no authoritative candidate-answer registration DTO. An answer remains an attributed Planning proposal for later Data Artifact publication and Research assessment; it must not be mislabeled an accepted answer. Keep its exact answer text, selected or local question identity, explicitly supplied method/context, source quotations and published interpretation reference. Missing method/context cannot be invented. The proposal wrapper retains ClaimGrounding and the originating candidate path even where Research's registration DTO has no provenance field. Model confidence does not become Research acceptance.

These proposal objects consume the same exact observed draft, byte-reviewed sources and finalized interpretation binding used by graph validation. They do not submit peer writes or reinterpret diagnostics as actions. Preserve quoted negation, contradictions and unknown quality. Batch limits and per-item dependency findings are explicit; neither oversize nor missing evidence silently truncates output. Question/need submission order and answer Artifact publication belong to B2.03, which must bind actual owner receipts before dependent accepted references are used.

Research currently exposes immutable question/need reads but no public declaration-write endpoint. B2.03 must add or explicitly bind an owner-controlled registration boundary before claiming automatic declaration handoff; Planning must never call Research private SQL. Method SchemaRefs in these candidates are declared identities, not discovered executable tools or verified scientific methods. Their actual availability/assessment use remains subject to owner capability/profile checks.

The exact published interpretation/source binding is common to both graph and Research proposals. Extract that behavior into a focused publication candidate object; Graph ProposalInputs adds Knowledge context, while Research validation adds question context. Do not force a Research-only proposal to acquire graph facts merely to verify its source provenance. A new additive planning.research-proposal-set/v1 contract makes proposed records, model attribution, exact grounding, findings and non-acceptance authority explicit without altering existing schema bytes.

## Aggregate repair authority

The existing BudgetLedger accounts per engagement; ExtractionTrace.prior is recorded after an attempt and cannot prevent a new engagement from receiving a fresh allowance. Introduce an explicitly enrolled repair family before a repair request can reserve or dispatch. One immutable root binds the original actor/scope, root request/task and reviewed BudgetPolicy. Root request CallLimits are the aggregate ceilings: at most its max_attempts (currently<=16), total tokens and accounting units across every member. Per-attempt ceilings and the reservation-floor charge profile remain unchanged. A repair does not increase those limits.

Family membership is immutable, unique per engagement and scoped to the root. A child enrollment names the exact persisted parent interpretation trace and child's registered task/request, before any child attempt exists. Verify the parent belongs to that family and its immutable source/target pins agree. Preserve original actor, objective, model/configuration, prompt, output profile, policy and action/limit ceilings. New diagnostic context does not widen tool/model/action authority. No automatic profile migration, scientific repinning or fresh root is inferred from a failed candidate.

The root may enroll an existing legacy root request only when its complete owned reservation history and exact stored budget policy can be accounted for. Count all prior reservations/settlements/late observations without rewriting their bytes or timestamps. Reject unbudgeted history. An already exhausted/overage root may retain/read its family record, but receives no new admission. A child that already has attempts cannot be retroactively enrolled. Legacy unrelated requests remain readable and retain their predecessor behavior; they cannot later attach a trace to an enrolled family while bypassing membership.

## Transactions and concurrency

Add new append-only owner tables through a new migration; do not alter applied planning_0001..0007. Retain exact root/member canonical bytes and registration timestamps, owner-scoped foreign keys and unique member identity. The service role gets only the needed immutable read/insert privileges. Readback reconstructs policy/task/parent bindings from this owner's records, never caller-supplied accounting totals.

For enrolled requests, acquire a family advisory transaction lock before the existing request lock. Reservation computes aggregate sums of the greatest reserved, settled and late-reported input/output/unit charges over all members, checks any known overage and total physical-attempt count, then inserts the existing attempt/reservation in the same transaction. A failed gate inserts neither. Existing reservations replay unchanged. Concurrent sibling repairs share these ceilings; branching lineage cannot multiply a budget.

Dispatch issuance rechecks family-wide overage under the same lock, so a reservation made before a newly recorded overage is not permission for a later call. Settlement and late-observation writes use the same family-before-request lock order to define a committed ordering with admission. A late report may exceed a prior reservation, and a remote call may outlive a local deadline; record that truth and stop subsequent admission rather than rewriting history or claiming cancellation.

Membership cannot be reassigned. Enrollment races with a predecessor un-enrolled reservation require a request-lock recheck: if membership changed after initial lock selection, abort that transaction with an explicit retryable membership-change result rather than acquiring locks in reverse order. If an unenrolled child already reserved, enrollment fails. The repair workflow enrolls before invoking the common reservation/dispatch path; enrolled requests cannot bypass aggregate checks by calling the predecessor runner. Trace recording rejects a cross-request link involving an enrolled family unless the child's recorded enrollment and parent family agree. No peer HTTP/model call runs inside these transactions.

## Bounded orchestration

A repair request carries exact machine-readable findings and prior interpretation identity as immutable context, not a prose claim that an earlier answer was accepted. Use the owned journal to retrieve parent/task/attempt facts, enroll the child, then reuse the physical dispatch/settlement boundary. Local retries and child requests charge the same family. Scope/action/configuration changes require a separately authorized campaign/root, not a repair shortcut.

Success, partial useful results, explicit abstention, aggregate exhaustion and unavailable evidence are distinct outcomes. Exhaustion leaves the best valid proposals and unresolved findings available for later campaigns. It never starts an unbounded new root or creates a routine human wait within the campaign. Human scientific corrections still apply between campaigns. Published findings/input context and actual owner handoff must be concrete before claiming a live integrated repair loop; deterministic fixtures remain explicit.

## Verification

Own tests under research_proposals, repair and persistence behavior folders. Verify distinct proposal types, exact quotation/context/method bindings, ambiguous and missing question/criterion targets, dependency withholding, stable IDs and no accepted-answer claims. Public Research reads require bounded authenticated transport and actual-owner proof before their origin is claimed.

Use actual retained PostgreSQL for concurrent sibling reservations at the final available slot, rollback, scope/actor rejection, duplicate enrollment, parent mismatch, old root adoption, child pre-reservation rejection, immutable readback, pending/unknown charges, later overage before dispatch and same-policy replay. Exercise membership/reservation lock races without reversed locking. Prove aggregate enforcement through the ordinary runner as well as the repair workflow. Independent installations/process replacement must preserve all original attempt/trace/charge bytes and the new family records. Scientific quality is not an admission veto, and no live provider call is needed for these software gates.
