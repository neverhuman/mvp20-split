## Positioning Decision

Pitch Amphora as a proposed research engine that makes the research pathway a versioned, executable and eventually evolvable asset, connected to explicit scientific knowledge and retained decision history. Its intended composition across materials, chemistry and biology is part of the product scope.

The differentiation thesis is the combination of reviewed scientific capabilities, separately authored knowledge and campaign graphs, evidence-bound execution, human inspection/intervention, and governed pathway evolution. Graphs, AI assistants, provenance, active learning, optimization and human review are not individually novel. We have not established that this combination is exclusive to Amphora. The commercial question is whether Amphora delivers it better for a focused buyer and research workflow.

Why build is a hypothesis about reducing campaign-change effort, preserving decision context, and reusing validated work across product programs. The implementation must demonstrate value beyond assembling available platforms and custom scripts. Buying/extending an incumbent remains a legitimate alternative until the pilot demonstrates a material advantage.

## Investor Claims and Evidence Boundaries

| Outcome-linked campaign history becomes a defensible asset | Potential moat | Rights-eligible retained outcomes, repeat usage and measurable reuse; no automatic pooling of private data |
| Product programs can lead to a platform business | Proposed commercial sequence | Sponsor/customer validation, delivery cost, repeat purchase and pricing evidence |

## Pilot and Competitive Diligence

Use a reviewed rod-depot representation and an oral-matrix representation to test software portability; synthetic objectives establish mechanics only. Separately validate any scientific evaluator with held-out data. A subsequent materials/biology fixture must preserve assay, species, route and endpoint context.

Measure campaign setup/change effort, decision reconstruction effort, fraction of reused validated steps, required-input/authority gate behavior, cost per reviewed decision, and scientific performance under matched data/compute budgets. Agree baselines and thresholds before the pilot. We have no measured superiority, customer traction, revenue, or fundraising amount to place on these slides.

Hands-on diligence should test the combined workflow against the closest materials and scientific-agent alternatives, including Citrine and Claude Science/Benchling, and include integration with specialist scientific and automation tools. Reconsider the build scope if extending an existing platform meets the requirement more economically.
