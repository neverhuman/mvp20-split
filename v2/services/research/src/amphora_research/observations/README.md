quantities.py owns DecimalValue and Quantity. DecimalValue accepts only bounded exact decimal text, preserves its original spelling and provides an exact Decimal amount independently of ambient arithmetic precision. Maximum128 characters,64 stored significant digits and exponent magnitude128, including the effective fractional exponent. No float/int/bool coercion, locale guessing, rounding or canonicalization occurs. Quantity owns a bounded printable non-whitespace unit token, preserving case/spelling. Unknown units can be represented; representation does not establish convertibility or scientific applicability.

values.py owns distinct NumericValue, CensoredValue, CategoryValue and MissingValue forms. NumericValue carries optional ReportedInterval uncertainty; absent uncertainty stays unknown. A reported interval has ordered same-unit bounds and a bounded description; no confidence percentage or statistical method is invented, and this module does not validate a point estimate against a statistical model. CensorLimit preserves exact inclusive/exclusive meaning; lower-only, upper-only and nonempty intervals stay censored, even if equal inclusive limits happen to name one value. MissingValue requires an explicit stable reason. Neither missingness nor censoring exposes a point quantity or defaults to zero.

series.py owns SeriesPoint and SeriesValue. Series have1..4096 immutable ordered points, explicit axis units, and numeric/censored/missing ordinates. Duplicate and unsorted coordinates remain intact. Every present point must use the exact declared units. No sorting, deduplication, interpolation, averaging or automatic unit conversion occurs. Empty/mutable series, malformed points, incompatible units, nonfinite text and inconsistent bounds fail with stable MeasurementError codes rather than changing source meaning.

These are private frozen domain values, not public DTOs or evidence authority. The pure value/identity layer has no I/O, SQL, source authentication, eligibility decision, Artifact publication or Runtime effect. Mappings preserve original text/units and explicit measurement variants. Versioned unit conversion, authenticated report ingestion and decision/correction storage remain later milestones. Measurement values depend only on Python standard-library modules; identity/record/matching objects additionally use public core/research contracts, Pydantic identifier validation and private Research experiment objects; other Research modules may compose these private values, and other services may consume only future public contracts.
