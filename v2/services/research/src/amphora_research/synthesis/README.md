# Research synthesis feasibility

- Status: BP-06.04/B1.04 domain objects and explicit public mappings implemented in Research0.16.0/contracts0.7.0. New scientific storage, authenticated ingestion and public APIs remain later milestones.
- Owner: Research. Design: [physical design](../../../../../docs/Build%20Plans/BP-06/BP-06.04%20Physical%20design.md); requirements: [synthesis feasibility](../../../../../docs/Architecture/Requirements/Product_Requirements.md#synthesis-feasibility-and-route-planning).

evidence.py preserves an exact candidate/domain/purpose/conditions context, requested scale and purity specification, immutable route Artifact, method/tool/model versions, source records, ordered step dependencies, step evidence/gaps, bounded search and next checks. Route-step records carry domain-specific intermediates, starting materials and process detail; this module does not interpret their chemistry or biology. Missing scale/purity stays unknown. Units retain original tokens. All sources match the candidate scope; steps must follow their dependencies and cannot form cycles. Collections are bounded, identities unique and sources attributable.

Evidence posture distinguishes unassessed, proposed, precedent_supported, documented_target, demonstrated and no_route_found. Route and per-step postures remain separate assertions. No-route reports require recorded search scope/budget and source evidence; they do not prove impossibility. Proposed, analogous, documented and demonstrated sources do not automatically promote one another. No route is silently discarded.

policy.py evaluates a RouteOption against a SynthesisPolicy's exact context, explicitly allowed whole-route and step postures, and named required execution checks. Each ExecutionCheck retains met/failed/unknown, evidence and reasons. A failed required check produces blocked; otherwise missing/unknown required checks or unsupported posture produce unknown; only satisfied declared gates produce eligible. Optional failed checks remain visible and do not independently block. Step gaps/next checks remain inspectable; only declared posture/check requirements gate execution. All decisions have fixed synthetic authority. A demonstrated route can be project-blocked; a proposal may satisfy an explicitly exploratory policy without becoming demonstrated.

assess_alternatives returns each option independently in input order. One blocked alternative cannot suppress another eligible option. No score, route search, precursor lookup, scale-up inference, unit conversion, implicit fitness penalty, lab submission, scientific authentication or Runtime mutation occurs. Frozen private objects use public core/research contracts and Research quantities. Other services consume versioned public contracts, never these modules.

State is entirely caller-supplied and immutable; there is no SQL or I/O. Artifact/method/policy references are claimed pins, not verified source content. Later adapters must authenticate sources and bind check assertions to their route/context before use. This module never decides campaign-wide progress or replaces an active pin. Malformed records raise SynthesisError; measurement parsing keeps MeasurementError. Tests/synthesis covers alternatives, unknowns, explicit gates, exact context, bounded searches, sources and step ordering. Select with dev/run_tests.py --module research --area synthesis. Replacement must preserve these decisions, provenance and limits before later storage/API integration.
