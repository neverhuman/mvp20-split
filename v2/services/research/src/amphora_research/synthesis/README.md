evidence.py preserves an exact candidate/domain/purpose/conditions context, requested scale and purity specification, immutable route Artifact, method/tool/model versions, source records, ordered step dependencies, step evidence/gaps, bounded search and next checks. Route-step records carry domain-specific intermediates, starting materials and process detail; this module does not interpret their chemistry or biology. Missing scale/purity stays unknown. Units retain original tokens. All sources match the candidate scope; steps must follow their dependencies and cannot form cycles. Collections are bounded, identities unique and sources attributable.

Evidence posture distinguishes unassessed, proposed, precedent_supported, documented_target, demonstrated and no_route_found. Route and per-step postures remain separate assertions. No-route reports require recorded search scope/budget and source evidence; they do not prove impossibility. Proposed, analogous, documented and demonstrated sources do not automatically promote one another. No route is silently discarded.

policy.py evaluates a RouteOption against a SynthesisPolicy's exact context, explicitly allowed whole-route and step postures, and named required execution checks. Each ExecutionCheck retains met/failed/unknown, evidence and reasons. A failed required check produces blocked; otherwise missing/unknown required checks or unsupported posture produce unknown; only satisfied declared gates produce eligible. Optional failed checks remain visible and do not independently block. Step gaps/next checks remain inspectable; only declared posture/check requirements gate execution. All decisions have fixed synthetic authority. A demonstrated route can be project-blocked; a proposal may satisfy an explicitly exploratory policy without becoming demonstrated.

assess_alternatives returns each option independently in input order. One blocked alternative cannot suppress another eligible option. No score, route search, precursor lookup, scale-up inference, unit conversion, implicit fitness penalty, lab submission, scientific authentication or Runtime mutation occurs. Frozen private objects use public core/research contracts and Research quantities. Other services consume versioned public contracts, never these modules.

Pure evidence/policy state is caller-supplied and immutable; those objects have no SQL or I/O. Artifact/method/policy references are claimed pins, not verified source content. Later adapters must authenticate sources and bind check assertions to their route/context before use. This module never decides campaign-wide progress or replaces an active pin. Malformed records raise SynthesisError; measurement parsing keeps MeasurementError. Tests/synthesis covers alternatives, unknowns, explicit gates, exact context, bounded searches, sources and step ordering. Select with dev/run_tests.py --module research --area synthesis. Replacement must preserve these decisions, provenance and limits alongside the storage boundary below; authenticated API integration remains later.

## Immutable synthesis decisions

decision.prepare evaluates the owner policy and derives a SynthesisDecision with all alternatives and their ordered checks, full policy/context, method/tool/model references, route/step sources, search scope, conditions and optional purity. Blocked alternatives remain beside useful ones; bounded no-route evidence remains unknown rather than impossible. The reference digest pins scope/kind/id, actor and the complete output/dependency document.

SynthesisStore.assess takes private reviewed policies and route options, never caller-supplied output verdicts. It requires the exact target candidate and atomically records the synthetic result and complete Artifact dependency index. It does not run chemical route engines, verify source content or interpret check assertions; trusted adapters must authenticate and bind them before this private call. No route-science or execution authority is acquired by persistence.

Both stores use closed Research-local decision journal mechanics in persistence, with research_0008 append-only grants. Exact retry preserves bytes/original actor; changed input/profile/actor under the same identity conflicts. Read requires exact scope/kind/id/digest and returns committed history without policy rerun. Source count is bounded at128 distinct Artifact identities; conflicting references for one id fail, never silently merge. Canonical output is capped at8MiB. A new decision uses a new identity; corrections cannot rewrite old decisions or campaign fitness.

DecisionIndex exposes bounded pages of dependents by exact observation revision or exact Artifact reference, plus explicitly registered immutable consumer Artifacts. Registration is a trusted private adapter call; public producer/lineage authorization remains B1.08, not inferred from supplying an ArtifactRef. It makes no global-consumer discovery or automatic feedback claim. Pages are a live append-only index, not a snapshot: concurrent insertions before a cursor require a fresh scan; later correction orchestration must account for that. Every page preserves future_campaigns_only policy. Rights/security remain separate.

Module-owned tests cover decision provenance, exact replay/replacement, dependency integrity and historical correction isolation. Persistence tests cover shared encoding/page/grant mechanisms. Local postcommit reply-loss probes are not process-kill evidence. B3/V1 own later process/API/replacement integration acceptance. Rebuilds must preserve full policy/result bytes, references, dependency pages, actor attribution and immutable consumer links; no active-campaign mutation or implicit scientific promotion is permitted.
