# Stage-2 results and exit gate

Completed the single frozen campaign and stopped. Both learners used six arms, four fixed configurations, three inner joint-component folds and five outer joint-component folds. All 1,560 fits including full fresh-process replay completed. Independent data/grouping, selection, metric/bootstrap/Holm and prefix-invariance audits pass. Model predictions, inner scores and selected configurations are byte-identical across fresh processes.

All Bao curves were development-exposed before this campaign. Every planned 95% conditional interval includes zero and no planned comparison survives Holm correction. Ordinary kinetic RF has the lowest point estimate (13.0774 pp); adding Pareto to ordinary features gives 13.1040 pp for RF and 13.5442 pp for LightGBM. Neither residual arm improves its corresponding direct point estimate. These are internal retrospective estimates, not external confirmation or an established Pareto-specific benefit.

All-arm tables, ten paired contrasts, software/data hashes and complete predictions accompany the report. The original pilot remains unchanged. No platform service, scientific authority boundary, production workload or remote repository publication changed. This closes the explicitly authorized benchmark exception; it does not reopen the platform build queue or authorize more tuning.
